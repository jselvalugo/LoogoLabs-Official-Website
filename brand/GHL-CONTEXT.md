## Core Feature Areas (for post topics)

### 1. CRM & Pipelines
- Visual deal pipeline — lead → prospect → booked → closed
- Contact records with full conversation history
- Tags, custom fields, and smart lists
- Activity timeline per contact

### 2. Automated Lead Follow-Up
- **Missed call text-back** — when someone calls and you don't answer, they get an instant SMS
- **Web form follow-up** — instant response the moment a form is submitted (usually within 90 seconds)
- **Multi-step sequences** — follow up on day 1, 3, 7, 14 automatically via SMS + email
- **Lead source tracking** — know which ad, page, or channel each lead came from

### 3. Reputation & Review Management
- Automated review requests sent after job completion (via SMS or email)
- One-click reply templates for Google and Facebook reviews
- Dashboard showing review count, star rating, and trend over time
- Alerts for new reviews so nothing slips through

### 4. Email & SMS Marketing
- Broadcast campaigns to your full list or segmented groups
- Trigger-based automations (tag applied, form submitted, payment received, etc.)
- A/B testing on subject lines
- Open rate, click rate, and reply tracking

### 5. Workflow Automation (the "engine")
- Visual drag-and-drop automation builder
- Triggers: form submission, appointment booked, tag added, payment received, missed call, custom event
- Actions: send SMS, send email, add tag, move pipeline stage, assign to team member, add to Google Sheet, send webhook
- Branching logic: if/else conditions, wait steps, time-of-day filters

### 6. Booking & Appointment Automation
- Online booking calendar synced to your Google Calendar
- Automated confirmation, reminder (24h + 1h before), and follow-up messages
- Reduces no-shows significantly (we typically see 40–60% reduction)
- Round-robin assignment for multi-staff businesses

### 7. Social Media Scheduler
- Schedule posts to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business, TikTok
- Content calendar view
- Post approval workflows for agencies or team review
- AI-assisted caption writing

### 8. AI Content Tools (60+ prompts)
- Pre-built prompts for: social captions, email subject lines, ad copy, blog outlines, review responses, SMS messages, video scripts
- Trained for local service business use cases
- No blank page problem — start from a prompt, edit to fit your voice

### 9. Courses & Communities
- Host paid or free courses inside the platform
- Membership area with drip content
- Community forum (like a private Facebook group, but owned)
- No Kajabi, no Teachable, no Skool needed

### 10. Payments & Invoicing
- Send invoices directly from a contact record
- One-time payments, payment plans, and subscriptions
- Integrated with Stripe
- Products catalog for recurring billing

### 11. Reporting & Dashboards
- Revenue dashboard — what came in, from which source
- Lead source ROI — which campaign/channel converted to revenue
- Appointment and booking analytics
- Email and SMS performance reports
- Custom dashboard builder

## Post Writing Rules

1. **Never mention GHL, GoHighLevel, or any competitor/parent platform name**
2. **Never mention Stripe, Twilio, Mailgun, or any underlying vendor** — these are invisible infrastructure
3. Call the thing "the platform," "the system," or "your marketing system"
4. Write from a position of practitioner expertise — we run these systems for clients, we know what works
5. Use specific numbers and data when possible (response time, no-show reduction, review count increases)
6. Always end posts with a CTA to book a strategy call
7. Audience: small business owners who are not technical — avoid jargon, use analogies
8. Tone: direct, practical, no fluff — like a conversation with a smart operator friend

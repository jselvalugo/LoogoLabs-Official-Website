## What this covers

text in `content/posts.mjs` and the copy stored in the `posts` table — is
licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0)**.

- Human-readable summary: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

We link to the licence rather than reproducing it here, so this file can never
drift out of sync with the authoritative text.

## What that means in practice

You may copy, republish, translate, quote, excerpt, and build on our posts —
including commercially — without asking us first. The one condition is
attribution: credit Loogo Labs and link back to the original post where it is
reasonable to do so.

You do not need permission. You do not need to email us. Take it and use it.

## What this does not cover

- **The site's source code** — the React application, Netlify functions, and
  database schema in this repository are not covered by this licence.
- **Trademarks and brand assets** — the Loogo Labs name, logo, and the files in
  `brand/` are not licensed for reuse. Attribution under CC BY does not imply
  endorsement by us, and does not grant permission to use our branding.
- **Third-party material** — we do not publish other people's work, so there
  should never be any. If you find something on our site that you believe is
  yours, tell us and we will correct it.

## Why we bothered writing this down

Copyright is automatic. An original piece of writing is protected the moment it
is written, in every country signed up to the Berne Convention, whether or not
anyone files anything or prints a notice. There is no way to simply decline it.

That means a site with no licence is "all rights reserved" by default — the
opposite of what we want. Saying "our content is free to use" in a policy
document has no legal effect on its own. This licence is how the intent
actually becomes true for the person reading it.
